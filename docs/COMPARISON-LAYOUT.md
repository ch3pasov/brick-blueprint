# Comparison layout

When a figure compares an official instruction with two rendered variants,
place the official instruction in the **middle**. Both alternatives should be
directly adjacent to their reference.

- LDView comparison: **LDView | official instruction | Brick Blueprint**.
- Parameter or material comparison: **variant A | official instruction | variant B**.
- Repeat that order in every row of a multi-example figure.

Move the complete panels together with their labels and measurements. Update
captions and directional references. Preserve the images' scale, crop, colors,
camera settings and measured errors when only changing the layout.

This rule applies to the research PDF and future comparison figures. It does
not authorize distributing official illustrations in the public source tree.
